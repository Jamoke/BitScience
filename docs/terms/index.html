Rifle Calcs

What it is. Rifle Calcs is a calculator. It predicts a bullet's path from the information you enter, such as your rifle, ammunition, scope, distance and conditions, using a mathematical model of flight. Its results are predictions, not measurements, and they are only as good as the information they are based on. Bullet data in the app is drawn from manufacturers' published figures, which can change. Weather comes from Open-Meteo.com, licensed CC BY 4.0, and may not match the conditions where you are.
