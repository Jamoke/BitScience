# Privacy Policy

**Effective November 13, 2026**

Bit Science Games apps are published by Jamoke Innovations LLC ("we", "us"). This policy covers those apps and this website. The first part applies to every app. Below it, each app has a section describing exactly what that app does, and that section takes precedence where it says more.

## The short version

- Our apps do not use accounts or sign-in.
- Our apps have no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share your information.
- What you create in an app stays on your device.
- An app goes online only to provide a feature you use, and its section below says exactly what it sends and where.
- We do not run servers that receive your data from our apps.

## Information kept on your device

Our apps save what you enter, and your settings, in the app's private storage on your device. Like other app data, this may be included in your device's backups, to iCloud or to a computer, depending on your settings. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us. Deleting an app deletes its data.

## Permissions

If an app needs access to something on your device, such as your location, iOS asks your permission first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings. An app's section below explains what it uses and why.

## Information Apple may share with us

If you have chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with crash reports and statistics about how our apps are used. These are collected by Apple, not by our apps, are governed by Apple's privacy policy, and do not identify you to us. App Store sales information also comes to us from Apple, without identifying you.

## This website

This website has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics, and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and like any web host, GitHub may log the IP addresses of visitors for security and to keep the service running. See the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply to you.

## App details

### Rifle Calcs

**On your device.** Rifle Calcs saves the rifles, bullets, scopes, profiles, drag curves, target choice and display preferences you enter. They are not sent anywhere.

**Location.** The app reads your location only when you tap the button to use your current position, or when you ask for the weather before setting a position yourself. The latitude sets the Coriolis correction, and the latitude and longitude are used to look up the weather. The position is kept, rounded to four decimal places (about 11 meters, or 36 feet), only while the app is running. It is not saved.

**Weather.** When you ask for current conditions, the app sends the latitude and longitude of your shooting position to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), a weather service, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The request contains no name, account or device identifier. As with any connection to a website, Open-Meteo also receives your device's IP address. Open-Meteo states in its [terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) that its server logs, which may contain coordinates and IP addresses, are kept for troubleshooting and to prevent misuse, and are deleted after 90 days. Weather data is provided by [Open-Meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com) under the [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) licence.

**Maps.** When you choose your shooting position on a map, the map on iPhone and iPad is Apple Maps, provided by Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). On Android, the map is provided by Mapbox (mapbox.com). While the map is open, the app loads Mapbox's map software and map images over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), so Mapbox receives the area of the map being shown, your device's IP address, and usage information that its map software sends to count map loads. Mapbox handles it under the [Mapbox privacy policy](https://www.mapbox.com/legal/privacy).

**Tilt and compass.** The app reads your device's tilt (for the look angle) and compass (for the direction you are facing) when you tap the buttons for them. These readings are used on your device for the calculation and are not sent anywhere or saved.

### Civil Calcs

**On your device.** Civil Calcs saves the values you enter in each calculator, your traverse sites, area figures and traffic counts (with the time of each vehicle counted), and your display preferences. They are not sent anywhere.

**No network and no permissions.** Civil Calcs makes no internet connections and asks for no permissions: it does not use your location, camera, contacts or any other information on your device.

**Exports.** When you export a table, points or a count, the app hands the CSV file to your device's save sheet, and it goes only where you choose to save it.

**Links.** The Website and Privacy Policy keys in Settings open this website in your browser, which handles the visit as described under "This website" above.
